Our Story

We matched on Tinder in December, 2021 and immediately started texting everyday, finally deciding to meet in person on February 23rd after Jade decided it was a perfectly safe idea to meet up with a stranger. We sat in a car outside of Jade's mom's house, talking about anything and everything for eight hours until Jade had to leave for work. Two days later, Jade visited CJ's apartment and never left.

Our first official date was at the 3 Michelin Star restaurant Olive Garden on February 27th. We were unofficially engaged weeks later on March 7 th with mall-bought bargain rings and officially engaged on June 17th that same year. We have already been taking on life as one for years, and will officially be tying the knot with friends & family on November 14th!
